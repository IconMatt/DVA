Transitioning from the ADF

  • Custom quick link This is a optional quick link and description

This is a WYSIWYG content section and styling options

Transitioning from the ADF can be both exciting and challenging. A strong support network helps you navigate change and stay connected. This guide covers practical steps, shared experiences, and where to find help locally.

Tap the Save button at the top of the page to keep this article in your bookmarks for later.

What you can do next

Small, consistent actions matter more than big plans. Pick one of the following and start this week.

  • Reach out to a peer support program in your area
  • Stay connected with mates from your unit
  • Book a wellbeing check-in with your GP
    • Mention your service history
    • Ask about a Mental Health Treatment Plan

Recommended schedule

  1. Identify two people you can call without notice.
  2. Set a recurring monthly catch-up.
  3. Review what's working after three months.

The biggest shift wasn't the gear or the title — it was learning to ask for help before I needed it.

John Smith
Ex-Serving Member

How can we help

General enquiries regarding DVA (not feedback)

For all general enquiries about DVA support, claims, and entitlements, call 1800 555 254 or visit dva.gov.au/contact.

For Open Arms Feedback

Share feedback about an Open Arms counselling experience by emailing openarms.feedback@dva.gov.au. Feedback helps us improve our service.

Fraud

Report suspected fraud relating to DVA payments or services to the fraud hotline on 1800 422 015. All reports are treated in confidence.

Privacy
