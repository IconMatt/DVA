Veteran and Family Wellbeing Agency

The Veteran and Family Wellbeing Agency helps veterans and families understand their options, connect with relevant services and navigate wellbeing support with more confidence.

Explore the Agency

  • About the Agency We help veterans and families find trusted support, practical information and clear next steps during and after service.
  • What we help with Support that reflects your situation, needs and next steps.
  • News and media Latest news, media releases, announcements and events.
  • Assistance and referrals If you would like assistance from the Agency or you would like to make a referral of someone who needs support.
  • Feedback form If you would like to make a Complaint, Compliment or Suggestion to us about the agency or website. This includes services delivered on our behalf by providers.
  • Get urgent support Access 24/7 crisis and urgent support services right away.
